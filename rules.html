Election Rules & Guidelines

Please read the following rules and instructions carefully before participating in the election.

1

Every student is allowed to vote only once.

2

Voting will be open for the scheduled period only. Make sure to vote before the deadline.

3

Multiple voting or any attempt to manipulate the system will result to disqualification.

4

Election results will be announced after the voting period has ended.

5

Keep your login details private and do not share them with anyone.
